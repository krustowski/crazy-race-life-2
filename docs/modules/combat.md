# Combat Missions

A briefcase-collection combat minigame: the player is dropped into a mission area (an interior, or outdoors) populated with armed NPCs and briefcases to loot, then flies a helicopter to an NPC "briefcase man" who pays cash for them. Mission layouts are data-driven from the database rather than hardcoded per mission.

**Source:** `src/modules/combat.pwn` (~650 lines)

## Overview

Mission geometry is stored in the `combat_coords` table, tagged per-row with a `type` matching the anonymous `COMBAT_COORD_*` enum (`BRIEFCASE`, `NPC`, `HEALTH`, `HELI`, `EXIT`, `ROOF`, `CP`, `NPC_MAN`, `DOOR`, `SPAWN`). `PrepareCombatInterior(playerid, missionid)` queries all rows for a `mission_id` in `id` order (a `ROOF` row fills in the `EXIT` pickups created before it) and, per type, spawns the corresponding pickup (`gCombatPickups[MAX_COMBAT_PICKUPS]`, up to 20), NPC (`gCombatNPC[MAX_COMBAT_NPCS]`, up to 25, armed with weapon `29` at `COMBAT_ACCURACY = 0.15` accuracy and infinite ammo), or vehicle (`gCombatVehicles[MAX_COMBAT_VEHICLES]`, up to 5 — currently only a model-487 helicopter). NPCs go through `CreateCombatNPC`, which names them from the monotonic `gCombatNPCSeq` counter (a name is never reused, since `NPC_Create` fails on a name that is still taken), skips the row when the NPC slots are exhausted or `NPC_Create` fails, and leaves unused slots at `INVALID_NPC_ID`. Each row carries an `interior` (`000016`): `NPC` and `NPC_MAN` rows create their NPC there, and the `SPAWN` row is where the player starts; pickups and vehicles ignore it, since pickups are not tied to an interior. A `DOOR` row also carries a target (`target_x`/`target_y`/`target_z`/`target_interior`), which the query reads with `IFNULL(target_interior, -1)` so that a door without one is marked `-1`.

`SetCombatMission(playerid, missionid)` is the entry point: it aborts any existing mission first, blocks if `gPlayers[playerid][InMinigame]` is already set or another connected player still holds the mission (`gCombatOccupant`: the pickup/NPC/vehicle arrays are server-global and every mission uses the same interior, so only one combat mission can run at a time; a holder who has disconnected or is no longer `Active` counts as a stale lock), otherwise calls `PrepareCombatInterior`, takes `gCombatOccupant`, flips `gPlayers[playerid][InMinigame]` and `gCombatMission[playerid][Active]` on, resets the player's weapons and gives weapon `26`(200 ammo)/`31`(500 ammo), creates an on-screen info textdraw, and starts a per-second `UpdateCombatMissionInfoText` timer. Last it puts the player at the mission's `SPAWN` row (`gCombatMission[playerid][Spawn]` in `SpawnInterior`); a mission without one (`SpawnInterior` = -1) leaves the player where they are.

Progress is tracked per-player in `gCombatMission[MAX_PLAYERS][CombatMission]`: `BriefcaseCount` increments on each briefcase pickup (`CheckCombatPickup`, `TYPE_BRIEFCASE`); reaching the "briefcase man" NPC within 4.0 units (`CheckBriefcaseManProximity`, polled every 2 seconds via `TimerBriefcaseMan`) pays out `BriefcaseCount * COMPAT_BRIEFCASE_DOLLARS` (50,000 per briefcase) and ends the mission successfully. `TYPE_HEALTH` pickups heal to full; `TYPE_EXIT` teleports the player out of the interior and shows the race checkpoint at the `TYPE_CP` point, the helicopter's destination (`ShowCombatCheckpoint`); `TYPE_DOOR` moves the player to the door's target point and interior, and does nothing for a door without a target. A mission played outdoors has no exit pickup, so the checkpoint is shown when the player gets into one of the mission's vehicles as the driver instead (`CheckCombatVehicle`, from `main.pwn`'s `OnPlayerStateChange`). `gCombatMission[playerid][CheckpointShown]` records that it has been shown either way, so getting back into the helicopter after the exit, or after reaching the checkpoint, does not show it again. `AbortCombatMission(playerid, success)` is the unified teardown: destroys all pickups, NPCs (only ids that are still `NPC_IsValid`, then resets every slot to `INVALID_NPC_ID`) and vehicles, disables the checkpoint, clears `CheckpointShown`, kills both timers and resets them to `INVALID_TIMER` (`TimerBriefcaseMan` is only armed on layouts with an `NPC_MAN` row), hides the info textdraw, shows a finish/abort gametext, saves a high score on success, clears `InMinigame`/`Active`/state, releases `gCombatOccupant`, and respawns the player (unless they died mid-mission, tracked via `gCombatMission[playerid][Dead]`).

## Missions

`ShowCombatListDialog` lists `combat_mission` by id and the response starts `listitem + 1`, so mission ids have to stay contiguous from 1.

| id | Name | Layout |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Planning Centre | Starts inside the Planning Department (interior 3): 8 briefcases, 21 NPCs, 5 health packs. The exit leads to the roof of a Downtown Los Santos skyscraper with the Maverick; the checkpoint and the briefcase man are at the LS docks. |
| 2 | Biohazard Labs (demo) | 9 NPCs and 3 doors in the objects at the end of the parkour course (`support/parkour.pwn`). It has no briefcases, exit or briefcase man, and its doors have no target, so it can only be aborted. Its NPCs were created in interior 3 until `000016`, out of sight of the player in interior 0. |
| 3 | Sherman Dam | Starts on the quay at the foot of the dam (5 NPCs, 1 health pack). The door at the quay's north end, where the game's own alternate dam entrance is, leads into the south end of the generator hall (interior 17: 7 briefcases, 12 NPCs, 2 health packs, on the main floor at z 4 and on the turbine platforms and west walkway at z 8). The exit at the hall's north end, at the game's `DAMOUT` entrance, comes out on the crest by the control tower door (4 NPCs, 1 health pack, the Maverick), 7 units north of that door so the player does not land on the commercial property pickup there. The checkpoint is the Verdant Meadows helipad and the briefcase man stands by the tower office. |
| 4 | Rockshore Building Site | Played outdoors (`000026`), on the building site in Rockshore East where the static Dozer is parked (`src/support/vehicles.pwn`). Starts on the pavement by the scaffolding on the site's east side. The ground-floor slab at z 10 (5 NPCs, 2 briefcases, 1 health pack, the west end under the first-floor frame), the open pit and the basement under the slab at z 4.3 (4 NPCs, 3 briefcases, 1 health pack), the excavation by the freeway embankment at z 5.1 (2 NPCs, 1 briefcase, 1 health pack) and the yard with its dirt ramp, skips, concrete pipes and pallets (9 NPCs, 2 briefcases, 1 health pack): 8 briefcases, 20 NPCs, 4 health packs. The basement is reached by dropping into the pit from the slab or down the dirt ramp from the yard. The Maverick waits at the yard's north end and shows the checkpoint when taken; the checkpoint is the helipad on the roof of K.A.C.C. Military Fuels (deck at z 35.5) and the briefcase man stands by the stairs onto it. |

Mission 3's points were taken from the game's map data (the `DAMIN`/`DAMOUT` enex entries, the generator hall collision) and each was checked to have a floor 1.0 below it and head room above. Mission 4's were placed the same way from the collision data: each has a floor 1.0 below it, head room, no wall closer than 1.6, and every pickup can be walked to from the start; the Maverick has 8 units of flat ground around it. Nothing in its layout is near the freeway's NPC taxi and truck tracks, which pass the site in the cutting to the north-west.

## Key Functions

| Function | Description |
|---|---|
| `public CheckBriefcaseManProximity(playerid, npcid)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:89`) | Polling timer that detects the player reaching the payout NPC and finishes the mission with a cash reward. |
| `public UpdateCombatMissionInfoText(playerid)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:122`) | Per-second timer updating the on-screen briefcase count / elapsed time textdraw. |
| `stock CheckCombatCheckpoint(playerid)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:142`) | Disables the race checkpoint and shows a "follow marker" hint while a mission is active. |
| `stock ShowCombatCheckpoint(playerid)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:158`) | Shows the race checkpoint at the mission's `CP` point with the helicopter hint and sets `CheckpointShown`. |
| `stock CheckCombatVehicle(playerid, vehicleid)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:179`) | Called when a player becomes a driver: on an active mission whose checkpoint is not shown yet, taking one of the mission's vehicles shows it. |
| `stock CheckCombatPickup(playerid, pickupid)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:197`) | Dispatches on pickup type (briefcase/health/exit/door) for a touched combat pickup. |
| `stock CreateCombatNPC(slot, const prefix[], interior, Float: X, Float: Y, Float: Z)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:261`) | Creates one armed NPC with a never-reused name; returns `INVALID_NPC_ID` when slots are exhausted or `NPC_Create` fails. |
| `stock ReleaseCombatNPC(npcid)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:297`) | Forgets an NPC id destroyed elsewhere (`OnNPCDeath`) so the teardown cannot destroy its later owner. |
| `stock PrepareCombatInterior(playerid, missionid)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:311`) | Loads `combat_coords` for the given mission, spawns all pickups/NPCs/vehicles and records the `SPAWN` point. Fails for a mission id with no rows, which would otherwise start an empty mission. |
| `stock SetCombatMission(playerid, missionid)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:471`) | Entry point: starts (or restarts) a combat mission for a player. |
| `stock SaveCombatMissionScore(playerid)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:531`) | Writes the player's briefcase count to `high_scores` on mission success. |
| `stock AbortCombatMission(playerid, bool: success)` (`src/modules/combat.pwn:562`) | Tears down an active mission (pickups, NPCs, vehicles, timers, textdraw), releases the occupancy lock and respawns the player. |

## Commands

The `/combat` command (`dcmd_combat`, `src/support/dcmd.pwn:1202`) requires admin level 1 or higher (`!IsPlayerAdmin(playerid) && gPlayers[playerid][AdminLevel] < 1`). If a mission is already active for the caller it aborts it; otherwise it opens `ShowCombatListDialog`, whose dialog response calls `SetCombatMission(playerid, listitem + 1)`. See [Commands Reference](../commands.md) for full details.

## Data & Integration

- **gPlayers[] fields:** `InMinigame` (read/write — blocks starting a mission while another minigame is active, and is cleared on abort), `OrmID` (read, for the high-score insert).
- **Database:** `combat_mission` (the list dialog), `combat_coords` (mission layout, read by `mission_id`), `high_scores` (write, `type = 5`, `spec_id` = mission id, `value` = briefcase count). See [../database.md](../database.md).
- **Integration:** `SetCombatMission` is invoked from `src/support/response.pwn` (combat list dialog selection). `AbortCombatMission` is called from `main.pwn` on player disconnect/spawn-reset paths and on player death (`gCombatMission[playerid][Dead]` is set before aborting so the player isn't force-respawned twice). `CheckCombatCheckpoint`/`CheckCombatPickup` are called from `main.pwn`'s checkpoint/pickup dispatch handlers, and `CheckCombatVehicle` from its `OnPlayerStateChange` when the player becomes a driver. `ReleaseCombatNPC` is called from `main.pwn`'s `OnNPCDeath` right before it destroys a player-killed NPC, so the teardown never destroys an unrelated NPC that has since reused the id. Uses `NPC_*` natives (NPC_Create/Spawn/SetPos/SetWeapon/AimAtPlayer/Shoot) and `EnsurePickupCreated` from `src/support/pickups.pwn`. Localized strings via `I18N_COMBAT_*` keys, see [../localization.md](../localization.md).

## Notes

- `PrepareCombatInterior` checks the NPC slots but not the pickup slots: a layout with more than `MAX_COMBAT_PICKUPS` (20) briefcase, health, exit, door and checkpoint rows together runs off the end of `gCombatPickups`. Mission 1 uses 15 slots, mission 3 uses 14, mission 4 uses 13.
- The constant `COMPAT_BRIEFCASE_DOLLARS` is a likely typo of `COMBAT_BRIEFCASE_DOLLARS` (missing "b"), harmless but worth knowing when grepping.
